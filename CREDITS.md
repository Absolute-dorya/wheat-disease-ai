# Credits, Licences & Attribution

Fill this in **as you go**, not at the end. It becomes your paper's
Acknowledgements section and protects you on both plagiarism and licence
grounds.

**Rule of thumb:** reusing *code* is fine with attribution + licence compliance.
Reusing *prose, figures or tables* from a paper is not. See [PLAN.md](PLAN.md) §7.

## Statements for the manuscript

- **Data availability:** where data lives, licence, and how to request restricted parts.
- **Code availability:** public repo URL + licence + commit hash at time of submission.
- **Ethics:** images of plants only; no human/animal subjects. Confirm any
  personally identifying metadata (GPS in EXIF) is handled appropriately.
